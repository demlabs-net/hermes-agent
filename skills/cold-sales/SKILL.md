# Cold Sales Skill

## When to Use
Use this skill when conducting cold sales outreach for DemLabs services.

## Target Audience
- IT companies in Novosibirsk
- Businesses with legacy software systems
- Companies looking to automate with AI

## Sales Script Structure
1. Introduction (who we are, why calling)
2. Pain point identification
3. Solution presentation
4. Objection handling
5. Call to action (meeting/demo)

## Objection Handling
- "Too expensive" → ROI calculation, comparison with hiring
- "We already have developers" → Specialization argument
- "Not the right time" → Urgency creation, limited offer
- "Need to think about it" → Follow-up scheduling

## Follow-up Schedule
- Day 0: Initial contact
- Day 2: Follow-up message
- Day 7: Second follow-up
- Day 14: Final attempt
